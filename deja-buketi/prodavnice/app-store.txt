It all started with one bouquet for my mom. She loves flowers but is allergic to them, so in April 2024 I made her a bouquet of satin roses that lasts forever. She loved it so much that I opened Instagram and TikTok the same evening, and three days later the first order arrived. Every bouquet is still handmade, with care for every detail. And the name? My name is Andrea, but as a little girl I said "Deja" instead, and the nickname stayed.
